You wake up as a mage, trapped deep within the confines of a goblin-ruled dungeon. Luckily, your butterfly companion has managed to set you free. It is now up to your imagination and your ability to control minds to help you escape!

### How to play

Download the latest version (jar file) from the [Releases](https://github.com/m4mbo/mind-weaver/releases) section or alternatively [build from source](#building-from-source). Also available on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3158220/Mind_Weaver/)!

### Controls

For more information on the controls, please refer to the 'Controls' section inside the game's main menu (ESC).

### What to expect?

* **Lights** and **particle effects** 
* *Pixel perfect movement*
* 5 levels of **increasing** diffculty 
* Brain wrecking **puzzles**

Unix based systems:

```

$ ./run.sh
```
