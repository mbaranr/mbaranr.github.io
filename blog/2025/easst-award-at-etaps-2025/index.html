I am happy to share that the paper Unsatisfiability Proofs for Horn Solving I contributed to has won the EASST Award for the best ETAPS 2025 paper related to the systematic and rigorous engineering of software and systems.

My contributions focused on developing an extension for the “Golem” Horn solver to enable the generation of verifiable Alethe proofs, helping strengthen the reliability of software verification tools.
