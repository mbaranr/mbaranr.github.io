        I am honored to share that I recently had the privilege of being the lead organizer for Lancaster University Leipzig's Qiskit Fall Fest 2023.

Qiskit, powered by IBM, is a robust Python library that empowers students and researchers to delve deeper into the practical aspects of the Quantum Computing (QC), enabling exploration at the circuit, pulse, and algorithm levels.

I would like to extend my gratitude to the following individuals and teams:

  • Parichay Sachdev, for his invaluable co-organizational efforts.
  • Ana Uznadze and Andrejs Bagdonas, for providing outstanding support during dynamic event sessions.
  • The team at Lancaster University Leipzig, for their valuable guidance and assistance.
  • Our esteemed panel of speakers: Aakash Ahmad, Dr Farina Riaz, and Enrique Solano, for taking time out of their busy schedules to generously contribute with insightful seminars.
  • The esteemed IBM team, whose vision made this opportunity a reality.

I remain motivated about the possibilities that QC brings to the table, and I hope that this initiative serves as an entry-point for students to explore it further.
