Mind Weaver

10 Oct, 2025

This game was developed almost entirely on my own as part of a graded project in my BSc in Computer Science Program. It was developed in libGDX and the art was done using Aseprite.

Introduction: You wake up as a mage, trapped deep within the confines of a goblin-ruled dungeon. Luckily, your butterfly companion has managed to set you free. It is now up to your imagination and your ability to control minds to help you escape!

How to play

Download the latest version (jar file) from the Releases section or alternatively build from source. Also available on Steam!

Controls

For more information on the controls, please refer to the 'Controls' section inside the game's main menu (ESC).

What to expect?

  • Lights and particle effects
  • Pixel perfect movement
  • 5 levels of increasing diffculty
  • Brain wrecking puzzles

Dependencies

Java 17 or above is required to run the game.

Building from source

Unix based systems:

$ cd scripts
$ ./run.sh
